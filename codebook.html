1 Overview

The American Local Elections Study (ALES) is a regular post-election survey of the residents of medium- and large-sized cities and counties in the United States, covering hundreds of local races between 2020 and 2026. Survey items have been grouped thematically, and are designed to capture residents’ preferences for both federal and urban policy, their experiences and satisfaction with public services, their evaluations of elected officials, and their vote choices for elections from county executive to president.

1.1 Variable Naming Conventions

Most survey items appear in multiple column variants. The base variable name (e.g., fedpol_build_wall) is extended with a suffix indicating the column type:

Suffix Meaning Example
_n Numeric, scaled 0–1 fedpol_build_wall_n
_n_r Numeric, reverse-coded, scaled 0–1 fedpol_build_wall_n_r
_oc Ordered categorical (factor) fedpol_build_wall_oc
_bin Binary indicator (0/1 integer) approve_mayor_bin

We use consistent abbreviations when constructing variable names:

Abbreviation Meaning
dem Democrat
rep Republican
inc Incumbent
countyexec County Executive
gov Governor
house U.S. Representative
pres President
sen U.S. Senator

For numeric (_n / _n_r) columns, values are scaled to the 0–1 interval where 0 represents the first response option and 1 represents the last.

Items are reverse-coded (_n_r) when needed using the following conventions:

  1. Policy opinion questions are reverse-coded so that higher values correspond to more conservative policy positions
  2. Non-policy yes/no questions are reverse-coded so that 1 corresponds “Yes”
  3. Answers that reflect quantities (e.g. on spending, approval, service grades) are reverse-coded so that 1 corresponds to the highest value (e.g. “Increased”, “Strongly approve”, etc.)

1.2 Missingness Conventions

The dataset distinguishes between four types of missing data:

Numeric columns (_n, _n_r, _bin):

Value Meaning
-1 Don’t Know – respondent saw the question and chose the DK option
-2 Skipped – respondent saw the question but left it blank
-3 “This office doesn’t exist in my city or town” (approval questions only)
-4 Not Shown – respondent was not shown the question due to question branching logic or because they did not complete the survey
NA Not Asked – question was not in the respondent’s survey wave or does not apply

Categorical columns (_oc):

Value Meaning
"Don't know" Respondent chose DK (only present as a factor level when DK was an option)
"Skipped" Respondent left the question blank
"This office doesn't exist in my city or town" Approval questions only
"Not shown" Respondent never saw the question
NA Not Asked – question not in wave or not applicable
