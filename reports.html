2025 Post-Election Reports

Fielded November–December 2025

In 2025, the American Local Elections Study surveyed 5,517 adults in 33 cities and counties holding local elections, along with a national sample of 2,346 adults. Each report below summarizes what respondents in one community said about their elected officials, local services, and the issues facing their area — from housing and policing to immigration and election timing.

For details on sampling and weighting, see the study methodology. Question-level documentation is available in the codebook.
